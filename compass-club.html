000° N  ·  Who we are
A compass, not a map.

A map tells you where to go. A compass tells you which way you're facing. We don't tell students what to become — we hand them the tools to work it out.

Compass Club is a student club at Al Yamamah University, founded in 2026 and running its first semester right now. Our members come from every major and every year. Most students graduate with a degree but without knowing their own strengths, and without the skills every job asks for and no class teaches. That gap is the whole reason we exist.

The person is always at the centre of the compass. That's not decoration — it's the point.
